RHEL e CentOSO Red Hat exige login para baixar a ISO (assinatura Developer gratuita): baixe o DVD e renomeie para o nome do catálogo. O CentOS Stream usa a ISO de boot e instala pela rede (kickstart aponta o espelho oficial).
Requisito do AlmaLinux 10Exige processador com AVX2 (x86-64-v3); confirme no hardware antes de instalar.
Por que o openSUSE não é automáticoO Leap 16 usa o instalador Agama, que o Ventoy não consegue automatizar com um perfil.

Linux

Distributions to install: Ubuntu Server, AlmaLinux, Red Hat Enterprise Linux, CentOS Stream and openSUSE Leap. Workstations in English or Portuguese; servers English only, with Netdata installed at first boot (needs internet).

{{LINUX_TABLE}}

Host names

They follow <prefix>-<model>-<system><language>-<d|s>, for example <prefix>-<model>-a10e-d (AlmaLinux 10, English, desktop) and <prefix>-<model>-a10e-s (server). The full list is in Machines and kits.

RHEL and CentOSRed Hat requires a login to download the ISO (free Developer subscription): download the DVD and rename it to the catalog name. CentOS Stream uses the boot ISO and installs over the network (the kickstart points at the official mirror).
AlmaLinux 10 requirementNeeds a CPU with AVX2 (x86-64-v3); check the hardware before installing.
Why openSUSE is not automaticLeap 16 uses the Agama installer, which Ventoy cannot automate with a profile.
